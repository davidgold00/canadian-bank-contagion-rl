## Unresolved limitations / separate proposed corrections

1. Editorial policy has no implemented reconciled allocation. Changing optimization constraints or manufacturing weights was out of scope.
2. ETF look-through is unavailable. The six-bank graph omits ETF economic exposure while retaining market-return risk. A future dated holdings diagnostic requires reliable holdings data.
3. Supervised validation needs a separate train-only label threshold, horizon purge, terminal-label correction, temporal repeats and baseline comparison. Existing reported results are preserved and qualified.
4. PPO lacks a dated training manifest and verified held-out boundary. Its training function uses the full supplied dataset. The training environment earns the current indexed return with newly selected weights/current-index features; inference and training return windows also differ by one observation. Proposed separate work: establish consistent information timing, record training provenance, then evaluate under a clearly new run identity. No retraining or retrospective performance revision was made.
5. Existing raw files cannot certify live-derived versus synthetic origin: the older downloaders can write fallback samples to raw paths without a status manifest. Proposed future intake improvement: persist retrieval/source status and input hashes on download. No existing data is relabeled as verified live.
6. No matched static comparison, calibrated scenario duration/likelihood, causal contagion identification or demonstrated robust portfolio superiority is established. Same-close execution remains idealized.
