# Current limitations

Use the [case-specific methodology](methodology.md) and [model card](model_card.md) with the published snapshot manifest. Superseded limitations are archived under docs/legacy.

- Download provenance is established for the new artifacts, not the earlier unverified cache.
- Historical adjusted prices and macro series are not a complete vintage/release-time archive. Macro inputs use an explicit one-market-session delay.
- Economic issuer/bank exposure through XFN/XIU is not assessable without verified dated holdings. Direct caps and the financial proxy do not establish issuer compliance.
- The composite score and scenario recurrence are configured research methods, not calibrated probabilities or causal contagion identification.
- Editorial ranges are guidance, not an empirically established investment mandate. A model-compliant proposal may be held for review.
- PPO does not explicitly observe the composite or graph in the frozen forty-feature schema. Its market-risk reward terms do not directly depend on allocation. Complexity must be assessed against simple comparators.
- A classifier predicting the site's own persistent score does not establish profitable timing. The persistence baseline is essential.
- The final period, three seeds, simplified five-basis-point execution costs, zero cash interest, and omitted liquidity/tax effects limit generalization. Corrected and legacy periods cannot be interchanged.
- Public users can check for published updates; only authenticated owners rebuild. External provider access and owner deployment configuration can prevent a refresh. The prior case remains available on failure.
- Automated and implementer visual checks do not constitute independent human comprehension testing.
