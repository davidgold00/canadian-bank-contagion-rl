Portfolio recommendation

Underweight banks broadly. XFN beta hedge worthwhile. Focus on lowest-node-stress banks. Use a 35–50% aggregate bank budget; 20–35%. Defensive positioning is justified by elevated tail risk. The primary trade-off is lower concentration and tail exposure versus potential participation in a bank-sector recovery. Because cash outputs diverge, the regime policy range is the decision guardrail and model-specific cash weights remain evidence, not automatic trades.

Why this recommendation

2 of the five inputs to the risk score are running hot: bank volatility at its 95th percentile and bank correlation at its 91st percentile.

Averaged against the calmer readings — financials drawdowndrawdownHow far an investment has fallen from its own most recent high, which is what an investor actually experiences as a loss. at the 72nd, yield-curve inversion at the 40th, and global volatility at the 35th — that puts the composite contagion scorecontagion scoreA single 0-to-100 reading of how much stress is in the Canadian banking system today, measured against its own history. at 66.7 out of 100, in the elevated band.

That score is not just a label — it is fed straight into the CVaR optimizer, where it inflates the risk the optimizer perceives in every bank position by 32%, so each bank has to earn a higher return to justify its weight. The optimizer also runs under a hard ceiling of 70% on total financial exposurefinancial exposureThe share of the portfolio held in banks and bank-sector ETFs — the part that would be hit directly if banks came under pressure. and a floor of 5% on cash.

Under those inflated risk estimates the optimizer settles at 51.3% financial exposure and 5.0% cash — comfortably inside the 70% ceiling, which means the risk score itself, not the cap, is what pulled exposure down.

The published guidance then tightens further than the optimizer's guardrail, because the score sits in the elevated band: a 35–50% aggregate bank budget and 20–35% cash.

That range is set by the band rather than by any single day's optimization, so it holds steady while the score stays at 60 or above; a fall below 60 would widen the budget to 50–65% and cut the cash call to 10–15%.
